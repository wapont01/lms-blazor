# Lesson 6: Final Exam and Renewal Readiness

## Lesson title
Final Exam and Renewal Readiness

## Overview
This lesson brings together the core Florida compliance principles covered in the course. Learners review the required steps for lawful practice, continuing education, and renewal readiness in a distance-learning environment.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:
- summarize the key Florida licensing requirements;
- differentiate between prelicense, postlicensure, and continuing education;
- explain why renewals and compliance deadlines are important;
- identify the role of the LMS in maintaining legal and professional accountability.

## Key concepts
### 1. Distance learning requires accountability
A learner may access content online, but course completion and compliance still depend on measurable work, required exams, and accurate records.

### 2. License maintenance is ongoing
Licensure is not completed after the initial approval. Florida requires continued education and active status management.

### 3. Provider responsibility is ongoing
The provider must maintain compliant course delivery, accurate records, instructor qualifications, and student support systems.

## Final exam prompts
- Describe why Florida real estate education is tied to licensing and renewal obligations.
- Explain the difference between a course being available online and a course being compliant with Florida requirements.
- Explain how an LMS can help support provider and learner compliance.

## Renewal readiness checklist
- license status verified;
- required education completed;
- renewal date reviewed;
- completion records retained;
- final exam passed;
- identity and exam verification completed.

## Quick review
- Florida licensing is rule-based and compliance-driven.
- Distance education still requires records, verification, and legal accountability.
- Education, renewal, and practice are linked.

## Knowledge check
1. What is the relationship between course completion and renewal compliance?
2. Why does a provider need records in an online course?
3. What is the role of the LMS in maintaining compliance?
4. Why is final exam readiness important?

## Conclusion
The Florida real estate learner must understand both the educational content and the compliance system supporting lawful practice. The online course environment works best when it is built around accountability, verification, and measurable completion.
