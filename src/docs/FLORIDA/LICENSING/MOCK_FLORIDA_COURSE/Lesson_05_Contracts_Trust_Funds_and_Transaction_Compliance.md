# Lesson 5: Contracts, Trust Funds, and Transaction Compliance

## Lesson title
Contracts, Trust Funds, and Transaction Compliance

## Overview
Florida law imposes strict obligations on real estate professionals in transactions involving contracts, funds, and customer protection. A licensee must understand how to manage transactions lawfully and responsibly, especially when funds are entrusted to a broker or sales associate.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:
- identify the role of contracts in Florida real estate transactions;
- explain the importance of trust account rules;
- describe the consequences of mishandling funds or records;
- explain why lawful transaction handling is part of professional competency.

## Key concepts
### 1. Contract basics
A real estate transaction depends on valid, clear, and enforceable agreements. Licensees must understand the need for written terms, proper signatures, and lawful performance.

### 2. Trust funds and escrow requirements
Florida law imposes clear duties regarding handling funds received in connection with a real estate transaction. Brokers and sales associates must follow the appropriate rules and records requirements.

### 3. Record protection
Accurate and complete records are not optional. In Florida, poor recordkeeping can lead to legal and disciplinary issues.

### 4. Professional responsibility
A licensee must not misrepresent facts, fail to deliver required information, or create legal exposure by handling funds or documents improperly.

## Activity
Explain why trust account compliance is vital to protecting the public and the integrity of the real estate profession.

## Quick review
- law and recordkeeping matter in every transaction;
- proper handling of funds is regulated and enforceable;
- clear contractual documentation reduces risk;
- licensee professionalism includes lawful execution of duties.

## Knowledge check
1. Why are contracts important in Florida real estate practice?
2. What is the legal importance of trust account handling?
3. Why must transaction records be accurate and complete?
4. What may happen if a licensee fails to comply with transaction rules?

## Conclusion
Florida real estate practice requires careful attention to contracts, funds, disclosures, and records. A licensee who understands these duties is better prepared to serve the public lawfully and professionally.
