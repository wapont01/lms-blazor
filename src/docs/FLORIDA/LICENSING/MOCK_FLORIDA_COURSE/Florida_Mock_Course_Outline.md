# Florida Mock Course Outline

## Course title
Florida Real Estate Distance Learning Fundamentals

## Course type
State-specific distance-learning course for Florida licensure preparation and compliance.

## Course audience
- prospective sales associates
- new brokers
- active licensees completing postlicensure or continuing education
- education providers building an online curriculum model

## Course structure
Module 1: Licensing Foundations
- Lesson 1: Florida Licensing Foundations
- Lesson 2: Distance Learning, Provider Rules, and Compliance
- Lesson 3: Postlicensure Education, Continuing Education, and Renewal

Module 2: Professional Practice and Legal Responsibilities
- Lesson 4: Brokerage Relationships and Disclosure
- Lesson 5: Contracts, Trust Funds, and Transaction Compliance
- Lesson 6: Final Assessment and Renewal Readiness

## Estimated duration
6 to 8 hours total for the mock course model.

## Completion requirements
- complete all lessons;
- complete module activities;
- pass final assessment;
- satisfy verification and identity requirements for the online delivery model.

## Florida compliance notes
- course is designed for online delivery only;
- provider, instructor, and records must meet state compliance expectations;
- course completion must be tracked and retained;
- renewal and licensing requirements are separate from course completion and must be monitored independently.

## State-aware design
This course is built so it can be used as a shared distance-learning template and then cross-applied to Florida and North Carolina state profiles.
