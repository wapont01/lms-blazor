# Lesson 1: Florida Licensing Foundations

## Lesson title
Florida Licensing Foundations

## Overview
Florida real estate licensing is regulated under Chapter 475 of the Florida Statutes. The Florida Real Estate Commission and the Division of Real Estate oversee licensing, education, and enforcement. A person who engages in real estate activity for compensation generally must hold the proper license and work under the appropriate legal framework.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:
- explain the basic structure of Florida real estate regulation;
- identify the major licensure categories used in Florida;
- describe the role of the Florida Real Estate Commission;
- explain why licensing requirements and education requirements are tied to the type of real estate activity performed.

## Key concepts
### 1. Florida real estate regulation
Florida law regulates brokers, sales associates, schools, and instructors. The legal authority is rooted in the Florida Statutes and related rules adopted by the Florida Real Estate Commission.

### 2. Core license categories
The main categories relevant to real estate practice include:
- sales associate
- broker
- broker associate
- school permit holder
- instructor permit holder

### 3. The Florida Real Estate Commission
The Florida Real Estate Commission is responsible for regulating the real estate profession in the state. Its role includes oversight, rulemaking, education expectations, and enforcement actions for misconduct and noncompliance.

### 4. Why education matters
Education is not just preparation for testing; it is also part of licensing compliance. Florida law requires course completion, renewal compliance, and continuing education depending on license status and time in practice.

## Distance-learning focus
This course is designed for online delivery only. Students are expected to:
- complete all required learning activities;
- complete work independently;
- complete exams and examinations under the required security and verification procedures;
- meet all course and renewal deadlines within the applicable time periods.

## Course requirement reminder
A learner may not operate as a sales associate or broker without holding the appropriate valid active license. Distance education does not remove the requirement to comply with Florida law and license rules.

## Important rule summary
- A valid active license is required to lawfully practice.
- Education and licensure requirements are tied to the learner's status and course track.
- A school or provider must hold the required permits and operate in compliance with the law.
- The education provider must maintain accurate records, truthful advertising, and compliant course management.
