# Lesson 4: Brokerage Relationships and Disclosure

## Lesson title
Brokerage Relationships and Disclosure

## Overview
Florida real estate law recognizes different brokerage relationships and disclosure duties. The relationship between a licensee and a buyer or seller affects duties, responsibilities, and disclosure requirements. A strong understanding of this topic is essential to lawful practice and compliance with Florida rules.

## Learning objectives
By the end of this lesson, learners will be able to:
- explain the major brokerage relationship types in Florida;
- describe why disclosure matters in real estate transactions;
- identify the difference between agency and transaction brokerage;
- explain how a transaction broker functions in a limited representation model.

## Key concepts
### 1. Brokerage relationship types
Florida recognizes several relationship models, including single agency and transaction brokerage. The type of relationship affects the duties owed to the buyer or seller.

### 2. Disclosure duties
A licensee must be clear about the role being assumed in the transaction. Disclosure protects the public and reduces confusion about duties and representation.

### 3. Single agent vs transaction broker
A single agent owes fiduciary duties. A transaction broker provides limited representation and does not act in a fiduciary capacity for one side of the transaction.

### 4. The public interest
Disclosure rules help buyers and sellers understand what the licensee will and will not do. In a distance-learning context, this topic is critical because the learner must understand the legal framework before practicing in the marketplace.

## Activity
Compare single agency and transaction brokerage in a brief paragraph. Explain which duties differ and why disclosure is required.

## Quick review
- brokerage relationships are legally significant;
- disclosure helps prevent misunderstanding;
- limited representation is not the same as fiduciary representation;
- licensees must know the difference before acting for clients or customers.
