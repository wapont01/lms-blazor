## Related License Information

Every corporation detail page exposes a `View Related License Information` control. Its public form posts an internal DBPR record identifier to:

`https://www.myfloridalicense.com/portalsearches/VerifyLicensee/LicenseRelation`

During this capture, the related-license modal opened but its partial content did not load. A direct request to the endpoint returned the DBPR generic page shell rather than a visible relationship roster. Therefore, this capture does **not** assert any broker, sales-associate, qualifying-broker, or supervising-broker affiliations for these corporations.

The searchable DBPR corporation data is suitable for firm identity, status, and expiration snapshots. Obtain a successfully rendered related-license response or an official DBPR downloadable roster before using firm-to-licensee affiliations for course compliance decisions.
