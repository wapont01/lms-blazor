# The CE Shop FAQ Research Notes

Temporary product-research notes based on the public FAQ page:
https://www.theceshop.com/faq/

Captured: 2026-08-25

These notes are paraphrased for product research. They are not a transcript of the source page. Verify regulatory and operational details before publishing them as LMS guidance.

## Observed FAQ Structure

The page groups questions into these broad areas:

- Popular topics
- General support
- Account support
- Course access and enrollment
- Real estate education
- Mortgage education
- Bookstore
- Appraisal education
- Contact or help escalation

The FAQ is designed as a self-service support center, not only as a licensing explainer.

## Question Themes To Consider

### General support

- What are the support hours?
- What payment methods are accepted?
- What is the refund policy?
- How can a learner request a reasonable or ADA accommodation?
- How can a learner contact support when an answer is not listed?

### Account support

- What should a learner do after forgetting login information?
- How can a learner reset a password?
- How can a learner update an email address?
- How are account or identity details corrected?

### Course access and enrollment

- How do I sign up or enroll in a course?
- How soon can I start after purchase or enrollment?
- How do I access courses after signing in?
- How do I continue where I left off?
- Can I use a phone or tablet?
- Which browsers are compatible?
- How are courses delivered?
- What should I do if courses do not load?
- Can a course be extended?
- What happens when a course expires?
- Can a learner print course materials?
- Do I need to complete every part of the course?
- What does seat time mean?

### Assessments and proctoring

- Does the final exam need to be proctored?
- How do I arrange a proctor appointment?
- What equipment, identification, or environment is required?
- What happens after a failed exam or assessment?
- How are assessment results and completion records handled?

### Completion and state reporting

- When is course completion reported to the state?
- Does the provider report completion, or does the learner submit it?
- What should a learner do if the state record is not updated?
- How does a learner receive proof of completion?

### Real estate licensing education

- Is prelicensing education available?
- What is postlicensing education?
- What continuing education is required?
- How does a learner upgrade a license?
- What are reciprocity and portability considerations?
- Can education count in more than one state?

### Business and organization support

- Are corporate packages available?
- Is bulk billing available?
- Can an organization manage multiple learners?
- Are custom or multistate packages available?

## Gaps Compared With Our Current Home FAQ

Our existing public FAQ already explains the North Carolina path from PB to Broker to BIC Eligible to designated BIC. The main missing areas are:

- Course enrollment and start instructions
- Course access and resume behavior
- Supported devices and browsers
- Course delivery formats
- Seat time and completion rules
- Assessment and proctoring instructions
- Course expiration and extension policy
- State reporting workflow
- Certificate delivery and verification
- Password and account recovery
- Payment, refund, transfer, and cancellation policies
- Accessibility and accommodation requests
- Support hours and contact escalation
- Organization, school, and bulk enrollment options
- A clear product catalog grouped by prelicensing, postlicensing, CE, exam prep, and professional development

## Ideas For Our LMS

### Public home page

Create separate expandable groups:

1. North Carolina licensing path
2. Course and account questions
3. Exams and proctoring
4. Certificates and state reporting
5. Payments and support

Keep answers short and link to the relevant course, login, certificate verification, or support workflow.

### Product catalog

Use clear education-path labels rather than presenting every course as an undifferentiated catalog item:

- Start your license: 75-hour prelicensing
- Complete your license: 90-hour postlicensing
- Keep your license current: continuing education
- Prepare for the exam: exam preparation
- Advance your role: BIC and professional development
- Support your organization: sponsored and bulk enrollment

## Caution Before Publishing

The CE Shop FAQ is a useful model for information architecture and support coverage. It should not be treated as the authority for North Carolina requirements. Regulatory answers in our public FAQ should continue to be checked against current North Carolina Real Estate Commission materials and current course-provider rules.
