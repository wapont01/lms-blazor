# Real estate entity campaign candidates

`Real_Estate_Entity_Campaign_Candidates_20261010.csv` consolidates business and entity-license records from the state research tracks: Iowa, Minnesota, Mississippi, North Dakota, Oregon, South Dakota, and Texas. Run `Build-EntityCampaignCandidates.ps1` from Windows PowerShell to rebuild it from the captured source files.

The current output contains 4,742 deduplicated state/license/type records: Iowa 23, Minnesota 37, Mississippi 21, North Dakota 20, Oregon 4,493, South Dakota 128, and Texas 20. Minnesota's 37 includes 19 supplemental individual-proprietor profiles and 13 Limited Broker profiles, kept in separate campaign segments rather than recast as ordinary real-estate companies.

The list is a research lead file, not an import-ready account list or a claim that every row is currently eligible for outreach. Use `CampaignSegment`, `SourceLicenseType`, and `SourceStatus` to separate target groups and screen status before use. The sample records are city-filtered except for Oregon's selected statewide Active Businesses roster and Texas's six-county Broker Company development sample. No email addresses or outreach permissions are supplied.

## Texas and Oregon campaign cohorts

- Texas contributes 20 active Broker Company records from the 2026-10-10 TREC roster snapshot, selected across Harris, Dallas, Bexar, Tarrant, Collin, and Travis counties. All 20 were opened and verified Active in individual TREC license-holder profiles on 2026-10-10. Eleven profiles displayed sponsored-agent counts (509 agents total); nine displayed no count and remain blank/unknown. TREC identifies the roster as a high-value public dataset and publishes instructions for importing/filtering it; those pages do not specifically authorize campaign solicitation. TREC's public-file instructions say phone, email, and mailing/physical address fields are withheld due to SB 510. Accordingly, these are internal campaign-research leads only: no direct outreach or account matching is approved by this review. Recheck status and resolve separate contact/outreach compliance before operational use. County is not treated as a street address, city, office, or branch; related-license references to organization officers do not establish parent/branch relationships.
- North Dakota's captured licensee-directory results show 38 active individual-licensee records affiliated with the 20 sampled firm license numbers; every sampled firm number has at least one such record. This supports affiliation/outreach prioritization only: individual status does not establish the firm's own license status. The firm rows still require a firm-level status check before outreach. The directory capture includes the corresponding licensee source file in `SourceFiles`.
- North Dakota's Bitz entry is explicitly labeled `Bitz Realty - Branch Office`; no parent firm/license relationship is established. South Dakota's reviewed official firm roster exposes firm name, address, city, phone, license number, renewal year, and issue date, but no current-status, branch-marker, or parent-office field. Its 128 nonzero license numbers remain firm roster candidates, not verified active firms or a branch count. Repeated license numbers/addresses are not used to infer a parent or branch.
- `Oregon_Portland_RBN_RBO_Campaign_Candidates_20261010.csv` is a first geography/segment cut of 945 records: Portland city entries whose published registration type is `RBN` or `RBO`. The 11 Portland `EA` records are excluded from this initial real-estate business cohort. The codes are retained exactly as published; the cut does not infer branch relationships, nor add row-level status that the source export does not provide. Rebuild the focused file together with the full campaign CSV using the builder.

## Inclusion and status

- Iowa includes captured active firm, branch, and trade-name licenses. The Miller & Clark branch is linked to firm F05469000 only because its official detail explicitly identifies that parent.
- Minnesota includes five detail-verified active companies, 13 detail-verified active Limited Broker profiles, and 19 active Real Estate Indv Proprietor profiles. The latter two are separate segments; personal names and small-business records are not silently reclassified as companies.
- Mississippi includes 20 detail-verified active companies and one active branch-office license. The branch's parent company was not confirmed, so its parent field is blank.
- North Dakota includes directory firm entries associated with the captured city samples. Active licensee affiliations are supporting evidence only; the captured directory results do not expose each firm's own current status. Bitz Realty - Branch Office is retained as explicitly named, but its parent firm is not asserted.
- Oregon includes the 4,493 rows from the dated Active Businesses export. That export is designated active but has no row-level status field. RBN, RBO, and EA registration types are preserved exactly, and no branch or parent relationship is inferred.
- South Dakota includes only firm rows with a nonzero license number, deduplicated by license number. The public firm roster does not display current license status or branch/parent relationships. The `LicenseExpiryOrRenewal` column holds the published renewal year for these rows; it is not an expiration date.
- Texas includes only the 20 strictly Active `Broker Company` records in the dated development sample; current status and expiration were checked against each linked TREC license-holder profile on 2026-10-10. Sponsored-agent counts are included only when the profile displayed them (11 firms, 509 combined); blank values mean not displayed, not zero. The official source review supports internal lead-list research only; it is not approval to contact licensees or match them to application accounts. The roster withholds direct contact and address fields, and its county field is preserved in campaign notes only as a source value.

Repeated source rows are combined by state, license number, and published type so the file has one candidate per key. Distinct names, addresses, phones, source paths, and notes are retained as semicolon-separated values. This does not treat repeated locations, addresses, or licensee affiliations as branches.

Use the state-specific capture manifests and lookup notes in each state folder for source limitations and verification detail. Do not infer a firm's Primary Broker, parent/branch relationship, or current status where the row-level source does not establish it.
