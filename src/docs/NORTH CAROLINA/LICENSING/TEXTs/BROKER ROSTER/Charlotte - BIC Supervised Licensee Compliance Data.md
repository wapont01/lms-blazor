## Source and Scope

The BIC supervised-licensee endpoint is:

`https://license.ncrec.gov/ncrec/oecgi3.exe/O4W_WRAPPER?ID=O4W_LIC_SLSM_LIST_NEW&LIC_NO={bic-license-number}`

It displays the following public, time-sensitive fields for licensees supervised by the selected Broker-In-Charge (BIC): license number, name, broker level, renewal date, Update hours, Elective hours, and Postlicensing (PL) hours.

This capture follows the first ten `CHARLOTTE` firm results documented in `Charlotte - First 10 Firms - NCREC Firm and Broker Roster.md`. Of the ten queried BICs, four returned supervised-licensee rows. The other six returned no rows at retrieval time: Lane A Holbert (132919), Lorenzo Marquis Robinson (317443), Kimberly W Young (184560), Phillip Auguste (245963), Charles Dennis Lee (236041), and Oscar M Agurs (114053).

Blank hour cells are preserved as blank, not converted to zero.

## Use for Course Compliance

These are NCREC display values, not an authorization to represent a licensee as compliant or eligible for a particular course. A production compliance workflow must, at the time of enrollment and again before issuing completion credit:

1. Re-query the live Commission record using the broker license number.
2. Apply the then-current rule for the broker's level and course type. `B` and `PB` cannot be evaluated under the same education rule.
3. Treat CE hour totals and renewal dates as indicators for outreach and eligibility review, not proof that a proposed course satisfies every state requirement.
4. Retain the exact query date, source URL, and the learner's consent/attestation with the enrollment decision.

The public records may change after retrieval. Do not use this dated capture as a substitute for live verification.
